About Odontix

Making dentistry easier to explore through interactive simulation and educational content.

Our Purpose

Odontix is the project behind Dental Clinic Simulator, an Android dental game created to let users explore dentistry through interactive gameplay. The project combines a virtual dental clinic, dental-themed cases, instrument selection and simplified simulated treatment workflows.

Our goal is to make dentistry-related concepts more approachable for dental students, beginners and people interested in dental education and simulation games.

About the Creator

Odontix was created by Dr. Taniya Gandhi, a Bachelor of Dental Surgery (BDS) graduate interested in dentistry and digital educational experiences.

The project brings together dental knowledge and interactive game-based learning. Odontix aims to publish useful learning material that explains dental instruments, tooth numbering, terminology and selected simulated cases in a clear and accessible way.

What You Can Explore

  • Dental instruments and their general purposes.
  • FDI tooth numbering examples.
  • Educational explanations of selected dental procedures.
  • Dental Clinic Simulator gameplay and simplified virtual cases.

Our Educational Approach

We aim to provide clear, practical and original explanations rather than relying only on short definitions. Learning content may include examples, instrument descriptions, tooth-numbering exercises and walkthroughs of cases represented in the simulator.

Dental Clinic Simulator is an educational game, not a clinical training substitute. Its simplified gameplay does not replace accredited dental education, supervised practice, professional judgement or patient-specific diagnosis and treatment.
