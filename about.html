About Odontix

A dental learning project built around clear explanations, practical examples and interactive simulation.

Why Odontix Was Created

Odontix is an independent dental education and simulation project. It began with a simple idea: make it easier for people to explore common dental concepts in a format that feels approachable and hands-on.

Dental Clinic Simulator brings that idea into a virtual clinic. Players can explore dental instruments, work through selected tasks and follow simplified treatment scenarios. The website also provides learning material for topics that benefit from a slower, more detailed explanation.

The aim is not to turn every dental topic into a game. It is to give learners another way to become familiar with terminology, instruments and the sequence of selected procedures.

About the Creator

Odontix was created by Dr. Taniya Gandhi, a Bachelor of Dental Surgery (BDS) graduate. Her interest in dentistry and digital learning led to the development of a project that brings dental subjects into an interactive format.

The content is shaped by a dental education perspective, with an emphasis on explaining what an instrument is generally used for, how a concept fits into a learning topic and where a simulated example has limits. The goal is to make the material understandable without presenting a game scenario as a real patient-care protocol.

How We Prepare Learning Material

Dental information can be easy to misunderstand when it is reduced to a one-line definition. Where a topic needs more context, we aim to explain the purpose of a tool or concept, describe relevant differences and use examples to show how the information is applied in a learning setting.

  • Use clear language and explain technical terms when they first appear.
  • Keep game instructions separate from detailed educational explanations.
  • Label illustrative cases as educational examples rather than real patient records.
  • Include references where appropriate and review material when corrections are needed.

Educational pages are reviewed and improved as the project develops. If you notice an unclear explanation, outdated detail or broken link, please contact us with the page name and the relevant section so it can be checked.

Important Educational Notice

Odontix and Dental Clinic Simulator are intended for general education and entertainment. The game and website do not provide a diagnosis, treatment plan or individual medical advice. They do not replace a recognised dental qualification, textbooks, current clinical guidance, supervised training or the judgement of a registered dental professional.

Real dental care depends on a patient's history, examination, investigations and individual circumstances. Procedures shown in a simplified simulation may leave out important clinical steps and should not be copied as instructions for treating a patient.
